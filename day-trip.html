Harajuku

Morning
10:00

🍜 AFURI Harajuku

Open from 10am, so you get the yuzu shio ramen with barely any queue. Counter seating, and you order at the ticket machine. It's cashless.

10:00 to 23:00 daily · Sendagaya 3-63-1 · 5 min from Harajuku / Kita-sando Stn

🚶 Walk to Jingumae

About 10 minutes across Meiji-dori into the Ura-Harajuku backstreets.

11:00

🛹 Supreme Harajuku

Be there when it opens. It gets packed fast and checkout queues build up by midday.

11:00 to 20:00 daily · Jingumae 4-32-7, 2F · ask for tax-free at checkout
11:30

🧥 Carhartt WIP Harajuku

Practically next door to Supreme. Good for workwear jackets and pants. Check sizing before you pay.

11:00 to 20:00 · Jingumae 4-28-25 · no exchanges or refunds
12:00

🔺 Palace Skateboards Tokyo

About 5 minutes' walk. Doors open at noon, so you arrive just as it opens. Collab weeks bring queues, and stock sells out quickly.

Mon to Fri 12:00 to 20:00 · Sat from 11:00 · Jingumae 5-9-20 · no try-ons
12:40

🚇 Chiyoda Line: Meiji-jingumae → Hibiya

Direct train, no transfer, about 12 minutes. Walk 5 minutes to Ginza 3-chome.

~¥210 on IC

Ginza

Afternoon
13:00

🐷 Tonkatsu Ginza Bairin Honten

Lunch. A Ginza classic since 1927, about 7 minutes' walk from Hibiya Stn. After 1pm the lunch queue is easing off. Order the kurobuta or the special katsudon.

11:30 to 20:00 · Ginza 7-8-1, B1 · ~¥1,800 to 3,500
14:00

👕 Uniqlo Ginza Flagship

2 minutes from Bairin. 12 floors, with lines you won't see in smaller stores, plus the full UT range. A good time to stock up on Heattech.

11:00 to 21:00 · Ginza 6-9-5 · tax-free counter in store
15:15

🍎 Apple Ginza

5 minutes up Chuo-dori. Bring your passport for tax-free. Japan-model iPhones have a camera shutter sound you can't turn off.

10:00 to 21:00 · Ginza 3-5-12
16:00

🚇 Ginza Line: Ginza → Nihonbashi

One stop, 2 minutes.

16:10

⚡ Pokémon Center DX

On Takashimaya East Wing 5F, with its own elevator from the lobby. Look for Tokyo-exclusive plushies and cards. The Pokémon Café next door needs a booking.

10:30 to 21:00 · Nihonbashi 2-11-2 · checkout line can run 20 min
17:30

🚇 Ginza Line: Nihonbashi → Omotesando

Direct train with no transfer, about 17 minutes. Take Exit B1 and walk about 5 minutes into Minami-Aoyama.

~¥210 on IC

Dinner

Evening
18:00

🍳 yellow Omotesando

Fluffy omurice that's sliced open on top of the rice, and the staff are happy for you to film it. Order from the English menu with the QR code. Save room for the French toast. Dinner is a good five hours after the tonkatsu.

11:00 to 22:00 daily · Minami-Aoyama 5-10-1, 2F · cashless · 10 to 20 min wait at peak, with seats outside

Good to know

  • Any day worksEvery stop is open 7 days a week. Palace opens at 11 on Saturdays and closes at 7 on Sundays.
  • Tax-freeBring your passport. Supreme, Uniqlo, Apple and Pokémon Center do tax-free on ¥5,000+ purchases.
  • CarryA foldable tote. By Uniqlo you'll be juggling bags, so consider sending them back to the hotel with a coin locker or takkyubin.
  • Food pacingRamen at 10, tonkatsu at 1, omurice at 6. Each meal is about 3 to 5 hours apart.
  • WalkableEverything in Ginza is within a 10 minute walk. You only need trains for the Harajuku → Ginza hop and Nihonbashi.
